Girivalam guide

Walking around Arunachala

Girivalam is the walk around the holy hill. Devotees walk barefoot and clockwise, keeping the hill on their right, and pass the eight Ashta Lingams on the way. The biggest crowds come on Pournami nights.

14 kmaround the hill
4–5 hon foot
8Ashta Lingams
  • Leave your bags and footwear in our free luggage room while you walk.
  • Carry water, and start after the evening rush for a calmer walk.
  • Come back to hot water and a fresh room, even at 3 AM.

FAQ

Good to know

Still have a question? Send it with your booking request and we'll reply on WhatsApp.

How far is the lodge from the temple?

About 7 minutes on foot to the Raja Gopuram, the main east entrance of the Arunachaleswarar Temple.

Do you have rooms on Deepam and Pournami days?

Yes, but they fill up early. Send your request as soon as you know your dates. A minimum 2-night stay applies on Deepam day.

Can a big group stay together for Girivalam?

Yes. Our dormitory halls take groups of 10 to 40, with separate halls for men and women and parking for your bus.

Can we check in late or leave our bags during Girivalam?

Check-in is open 24 hours and check-out is 24 hours after you arrive. The luggage room is free for guests.

Is there parking for cars and tourist buses?

Yes. Cars and vans park at the lodge, and there is space for tourist buses nearby.

Do you help foreign guests going to Ramana Ashram?

Yes. Our reception speaks English and books autos to Sri Ramana Ashram and the other ashrams on the Girivalam road.
