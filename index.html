Girivalam guide

Walking around Arunachala

Girivalam is the 14 km walk around the holy hill. Most devotees walk barefoot, clockwise, starting from the temple. The walk takes about 4 to 5 hours, and the biggest crowds come on Pournami (full moon) nights.

On the way you pass the eight Ashta Lingams:

  • Indra
  • Agni
  • Yama
  • Niruthi
  • Varuna
  • Vayu
  • Kubera
  • Esanya
  • 🎒 Leave your bags and footwear in our free luggage room while you walk.
  • 💧 Carry water and start after the evening rush for a calmer walk.
  • ♨️ Come back to hot water and a fresh room, even at 3 AM.

Location

How to reach Tiruvannamalai

FAQ

Good to know

How far is the lodge from Arunachaleswarar Temple?

About 7 minutes on foot to the Raja Gopuram, the main east entrance of the temple.

Do you have rooms on Karthigai Deepam and Pournami days?

Yes, but they fill up early. Send your booking request as soon as you know your dates. A minimum 2-night stay applies on Deepam day.

Can a big group stay together for Girivalam?

Yes. Our dormitory halls take groups of 10 to 40 people, with separate halls for men and women and parking for your bus.

Can we check in late at night or leave our bags during Girivalam?

Check-in is open 24 hours. Our luggage room is free for guests, so you can walk Girivalam and come back to a hot bath.

Is there parking for cars and tourist buses?

Yes. We have parking for cars and vans at the lodge and space for tourist buses nearby.

Do you help foreign guests visiting Ramana Ashram?

Yes. Our reception speaks English and can book autos to Sri Ramana Ashram and other ashrams on the Girivalam road.
